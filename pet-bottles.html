How PET bottles are made

From preform to bottle.

01

Preform

Every bottle starts as a PET preform — a small, thick tube with the screw neck already formed.

02

Heating

Preforms travel past infrared heaters until the plastic is soft enough to stretch.

03

Stretch & blow

Inside the mould, a rod stretches the warm preform and high-pressure air blows it out into the bottle's shape.

04

Cool & release

The bottle cools against the mould walls, sets into its square, ribbed form and is released.

05

Check & pack

Finished bottles are checked and packed for dispatch — clean, empty and ready to fill.
