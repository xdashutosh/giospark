How every bottle is made

From source
to sealed.

GIO Spark packaged drinking water is processed using Reverse Osmosis, UV treatment and ozonation, with minerals added as part of our product offering.

01

Reverse Osmosis

Water is pushed through fine semi-permeable membranes that hold back dissolved solids and impurities.

Purification
02

UV Treatment

Ultraviolet light is used as a disinfection step, helping inactivate microorganisms without adding chemicals.

Disinfection
03

Ozonation

Ozone is used as a further disinfection step before bottling, helping keep every bottle fresh.

About GIO Spark

Meet GIO Spark — refreshment for every moment.

GIO Spark is a packaged drinking water brand from Chaudhary Enterprises, created to bring refreshing hydration to everyday life.

With a focus on product quality, responsible processing and customer trust, GIO Spark aims to serve consumers, retailers, offices, hospitality businesses and distribution partners.

Our packaged drinking water is processed using Reverse Osmosis (RO), Ultraviolet (UV) treatment and ozonation, with added minerals as part of our product offering — a convenient hydration choice for everyday routines, travel, workplaces, gatherings and events.
